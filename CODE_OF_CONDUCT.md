# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is an academic unsupervised learning submission by **THE PARTH SHAH** at Red & White Skill Education.  
All contributors and visitors are expected to engage respectfully and constructively.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and learning community

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Plagiarism or presenting this work as your own without attribution
- Any other unprofessional conduct
